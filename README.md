# Quantitative Economics and Finance

## 1. Research portfolio

I study what derivative prices reveal about risk premia in macro-financial markets, and what they cannot reveal without further assumptions. Each project has its own repository. Each states its question and estimands before estimation, derives the results it relies on, implements them with independent numerical checks, and reports its findings with their uncertainty and limitations.
