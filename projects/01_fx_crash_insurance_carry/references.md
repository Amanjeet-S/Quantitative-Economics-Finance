# References

Every source cited in the project, what it is used for, and the version consulted. Bibliographic details were checked against Crossref, OpenAlex, publisher pages, JSTOR or Project Euclid on 24 September 2026. "Used for" states the claim or method taken from the source. "Consulted" states what was actually read. Where only an abstract or an earlier version was read, the text says so. No source content is used without citation.
